# THE COFFEE SHOP — META JOBS (6 launch drinks, lettering locked)

Launch drinks: Mocha, Espresso, Chai, Coffee, Matcha, Iced Caramel. Dog drinks: Latte, Pup Cup. Later waves (not in this pack): Cold Brew, Vanilla, White Mocha, Green Tea, Iced Green Tea.

## THE LOCK (same on every image)

- Hoodie color: **Roast brown #4D2E1F** for Mocha, Espresso, Chai, Coffee and Iced Caramel. **Matcha green #6B8667** for Matcha. One brown for all five. Chai must not be lighter than the rest.
- Lettering: **thick cream #EFE8DC varsity block capitals, raised twill appliqué with a stitched edge.** Never thin serif embroidery.
- One-word names sit on one arch. Two-word names (Iced Caramel) go on two lines: first word arched, second word straight below, same letter height.
- Dog hoodie lettering: same style, half the size, one arch across the upper back, fully visible.
- Backdrop: warm cream-greige **#E9E4D8**, every image. Format: **4:5 portrait**, every image.
- No text on cups, no brand names, no logos. Fronts are plain.
- Models are seen from behind, face never visible.

## How to run

Upload the INPUT file(s) as Image 1 (and Image 2, 3 where listed), paste the PROMPT, save the result with the exact SAVE AS name into `jobs/inbox/`. Your reference images are in `reference/coffee-shop/`; the two style locks are **COFFEE-REF-MOCHA-FLAT-v1.jpg** (brown) and **COFFEE-REF-MATCHA-FLAT-v1.jpg** (green). Jobs marked RUN AFTER use an approved result from `jobs/done/`, so approve P1 first.

**Rerun the job if:** a letter is wrong or missing, the lettering style changes (thin or serif), the color drifts, text appears on a cup, the dog's lettering is hidden or cut off, or the picture is not 4:5 on the warm backdrop.

**Your earlier 10 images:** Mocha flat and Matcha flat are the style locks and stay as they are. The Chai flat, both Caramel images and the Mocha and Caramel model shots are marked REDO in `reference/coffee-shop/` because of lettering style, clipped dog lettering or stacked layout. Espresso and Coffee flats are good but landscape and a different backdrop, so they are replaced by the 4:5 versions below (they can still go on the page as an extra image).
